Read this before you quote. Figures are historical observations from one NOAA GHCN-Daily station and are not a forecast or a guarantee of future snowfall, profit, or contract outcomes. A "storm event" here is consecutive days with ≥0.1 in of new snow, summed; daily snowfall is measured once per day, so timing of storms can split or merge events. Ice, freezing rain, drifting and refreeze are not captured. Stations can be miles from a property and snowfall varies locally (lake effect, elevation). Missing days are treated as no snow; seasons with less than 90% of Nov–Mar days reported are excluded. Use this as one input alongside your own site visit, costs and judgment. Not legal, insurance or financial advice.
