Seasonal contract pricing worksheet (one property)

A. Trigger depth in your agreement (in)
B. Planning storm count at that trigger (use P80 from the table or tool)
C. Extra pushes per season for long storms (estimate)
D. Total pushes to price (B + C)
E. Your all-in cost per push ($): labor + equipment + fuel + travel + overhead
F. Salt applications to price (e.g. median/P80 snow days ≥0.1")
G. Your cost per salt application ($)
H. Season cost = D×E + F×G ($)
I. Target margin (e.g. 0.25)
J. Seasonal price = H ÷ (1 − I) ($)
K. Monthly billing = J ÷ billing months
L. Check the worst winter: max storms × E + salts × G. Would J still cover it?
M. If not, offer a hybrid: fee for the median winter + per-push overage
