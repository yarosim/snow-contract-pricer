Privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This site is a set of static pages about the Seasonal Snow Contract Pricer. It has no user accounts and no login. The signup and feedback forms send what you enter to the site owner.

What we collect

  • Signup and feedback forms. The free-table signup asks for your email. The feedback form asks for a message and an optional email. Those forms send the email and message you enter to the site owner through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use that only to send the requested resource and to reply. We never sell it.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Stripe collects your email address and payment details. We receive your name, email, the product you bought and the amount. We never see or store your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply and handle support or refund requests.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site, and we don't sell your personal information.

How we use it

Only to send the resource you asked for, reply to you, deliver your purchase, provide support, process refunds, and keep required business and tax records.

The tool itself

The pricer runs entirely in your browser, and the downloaded version works offline. ZIP codes, costs and other values you enter are not sent to us.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have a signup or feedback submission deleted (except records we must keep by law), email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com.
