How many snowstorms does Binghamton, NY get per winter? (1996–2026 data)

At NOAA station USW00004725 (Binghamton, NY), there were 30 complete winters of snowfall records between 1996-97 and 2025-26. Median seasonal snowfall was 81.7". Storms totaling 1"+: median 16 per winter (range 7–23). Storms of 2"+: median 12 (80th percentile 13.0; range 5–17).
