How many snowstorms does Madison Dane Co Rgnl Ap, WI get per winter? (1996–2026 data)

At NOAA station USW00014837 (Madison Dane Co Rgnl Ap, WI), there were 30 complete winters of snowfall records between 1996-97 and 2025-26. Median seasonal snowfall was 46.75". Storms totaling 1"+: median 10.5 per winter (range 6–22). Storms of 2"+: median 7 (80th percentile 10.2; range 4–16).
