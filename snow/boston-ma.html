How many snowstorms does Boston, MA get per winter? (1996–2026 data)

At NOAA station USW00014739 (Boston, MA), there were 30 complete winters of snowfall records between 1996-97 and 2025-26. Median seasonal snowfall was 39.7". Storms totaling 1"+: median 7.5 per winter (range 3–14). Storms of 2"+: median 6 (80th percentile 8.0; range 1–12).
