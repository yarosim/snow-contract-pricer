How many snowstorms does Hartford-Bradley Intl Ap, CT get per winter? (1996–2026 data)

At NOAA station USW00014740 (Hartford-Bradley Intl Ap, CT), there were 25 complete winters of snowfall records between 1996-97 and 2025-26. Median seasonal snowfall was 45.6". Storms totaling 1"+: median 9 per winter (range 4–16). Storms of 2"+: median 6 (80th percentile 8.2; range 3–12).
