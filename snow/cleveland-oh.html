How many snowstorms does Cleveland, OH get per winter? (1996–2026 data)

At NOAA station USW00014820 (Cleveland, OH), there were 30 complete winters of snowfall records between 1996-97 and 2025-26. Median seasonal snowfall was 53.1". Storms totaling 1"+: median 12.5 per winter (range 7–20). Storms of 2"+: median 8 (80th percentile 11.0; range 5–16).
